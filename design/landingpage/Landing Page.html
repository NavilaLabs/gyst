Why zeitrak

Built different.
Built for you.

No subscriptions. No data harvesting. No lock-in. Just a meticulously crafted tool you actually own.

Activity-first tracking

Every tracked session is tied to a named activity. Build a clear picture of where your hours actually go — no loose timers, no orphaned entries.

Sidetrack distractions

When you get pulled away, don't stop — sidetrack. Interruptions appear as a parallel track in the log, keeping your main line unbroken and honest.

Local-first, always yours

Your data lives on your machine. Self-host for teams or run it offline — zeitrak works without a cloud dependency, because your work history is yours.

Event-sourced history

Every action — start, pause, sidetrack, resume — is stored as an immutable event. You get a complete, auditable log with perfect replay and no data loss.

Plugin ecosystem

Extend zeitrak with community and official plugins. Worktime, DATEV export, project management — powerful add-ons that slot right in, all free.

Rich reports

Visualize your week as a train journey. Drill into activities, spot distractions, and export to CSV — or let the DATEV plugin handle accounting exports.

Track Map

Your day as a
railway.

The Track Map is the heart of zeitrak. Activities appear as stations on a timeline. You see not just what you worked on, but the exact rhythm and shape of your day.

Stations & milestones
Each activity becomes a station on your rail. Complete it, and the train moves on.
Parallel sidetracks
Distractions branch off as a dashed parallel track — always visible, never lost, never pretending they didn't happen.
Immutable event log
Powered by event sourcing — every action is a logged event. The Track Map is just one view of your complete history.
Event Log

Every moment,
recorded.

Built on event sourcing — every start, pause, sidetrack, and resume is an immutable event. You get a complete audit trail, time-travel debugging, and perfect data integrity.

Why event sourcing?
✓ Automatic, complete audit trail
✓ Replay and reconstruct any state
✓ Zero data loss, even on crash
✓ Plugins can subscribe to any event
